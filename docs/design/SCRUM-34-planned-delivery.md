# SCRUM-34 — planned delivery production gate

The existing delivery zone, polygon, dated rider capacity, accepted price, rider hold and inventory hold remain authoritative. Future delivery adds an independent `GOKUL_FEATURES_PLANNED_DELIVERY_PRODUCTION` flag, default OFF. OFF returns no future delivery windows, while same-day delivery retains its existing checks. This does not enable delivery checkout on its own; all delivery prerequisites still apply.

With the flag ON, every requested item must have online-enabled `DAILY_PRODUCTION` policy and an approved or ready dated allocation with enough quantity after buffer, held, committed and wasted amounts. An `expected_ready_at` is required and must be at or before the selected rider window start. Production lead time must also fit before that window in IST. Quote results are provisional. The checkout transaction rechecks the production plan after locking allocation rows and reserves stock and rider capacity atomically; failed rechecks roll back the order. A changed address, date or cart requires a new quote. The customer delivery form keeps the cart when a window is unavailable so the customer can change details or choose pickup.

QA on DEV: with the flag OFF verify no future delivery windows and same-day fallback. With it ON, test approved quantity versus forecast, mixed products and lead times, a window before and after expected readiness, exact zone and reviewed pin, paused/full rider windows, parallel last-capacity checkouts, IST midnight, staff reducing or delaying production during checkout, payment timeout and cancellation/refund release. Verify a changed address/date requotes without losing cart. Keep both planned delivery and checkout flags OFF until these and provider/staff flows pass; no live DEV evidence is claimed here.

Reused future-delivery inventory holds also lock and recheck the current approval/readiness status and supply backing all held and committed quantities, safety buffer and wastage. A withdrawn or reduced plan rejects checkout without changing the hold. A valid last-unit hold is reusable without requiring spare stock.
